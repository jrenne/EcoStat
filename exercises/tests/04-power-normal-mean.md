## Statement

Let $X_1,\dots,X_n$ be i.i.d. $\mathcal{N}(\mu,4)$. Consider $H_0:\mu=0$ against $H_1:\mu>0$. The test rejects when $\bar X_n>c_n$.

1. For $n=25$, choose $c_n$ so that the test has size $5\%$.
2. Compute the power of this test when $\mu=0.5$.
3. Keeping a 5% significance level, find the smallest integer $n$ that gives power of at least $90\%$ when $\mu=0.5$.
4. Explain why reducing the significance level while holding $n$ fixed reduces power at every fixed alternative $\mu>0$.
