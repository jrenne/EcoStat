## Statement
Assume that $Z|Y \sim \mathcal{N}(0, Y^2)$ and that $Y \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$.

1.  Compute $\mathbb{V}ar(Z)$.

2.  Recalling that the kurtosis of a normal variable is equal to 3, compute $\mathbb{E}(Y^4)$.

3.  Compute $\mathbb{E}(Z^4 | Y)$ and deduce $\mathbb{E}(Z^4)$.

4.  Compute the kurtosis of $Z$.

5.  Is $Z$ a Gaussian variable?
