## Statement
*Verbeek (2004), exercise 2.4 (slightly modified)*

Carefully read the following statements. Are they True or False? Explain.

1.  Under Gauss-Markov conditions, OLS can be shown to be BLUE. The phrase linear in this acronym refers to the fact that we are estimating a linear model.

2.  The Gauss--Markov conditions alone are sufficient for the usual exact *t*-test in small samples.

3.  A regression of the OLS residual upon the regressors included in the model by construction yields an $R^2$ of zero. (We suppose the regression includes a constant.)

4.  The hypothesis that the OLS estimator is equal to zero can be tested by means of a *t*-test.

5.  If the absolute *t*-value of a coefficient is smaller than 1.96, we reject the null hypothesis that the coefficient is zero, with 95% confidence.

6.  Because OLS provides the *best* linear approximation of a variable $y$ from a set of regressors, OLS also gives the *best* linear unbiased estimators for the coefficients of the regressors.

7.  If a variable in a model is significant at the 10% level, it is also significant at the 5% level.
