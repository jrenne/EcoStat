## Statement
Among the processes defined by the following difference equations, which ones admit a causal covariance-stationary solution? (We assume that $\varepsilon_t \sim i.i.d.\,\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$.)

1.  $y_t = 1.1 y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t$

2.  $y_t = 1.7 y_{t-1} - 0.3 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t$

3.  $y_t = 1.3 y_{t-1} - 0.42 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t$
