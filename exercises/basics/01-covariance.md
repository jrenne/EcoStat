## Statement

Let $X,Y,V$ have finite second moments, and let $a,b,c$ be constants. Write $\mu_X=\mathbb{E}(X)$ and $\sigma_{XY}=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$.

(a) Show that $\operatorname{Cov}(a+bX+cV,Y)=b\sigma_{XY}+c\sigma_{VY}$.
(b) Show that $\mathbb{E}(XY)=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)+\mathbb{E}(X)\mathbb{E}(Y)$.
