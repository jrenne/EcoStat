## Statement

Let $U$ be uniform on $[0,1]$, and suppose $Y\mid U\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(U)$.

For each statement, decide whether it is true or false and justify your answer.

1. $\mathbb{E}(Y\mid U)=U$.
2. $\mathbb{V}ar(Y\mid U)=U^2$.
3. $\mathbb{V}ar(Y)=1/4$.
4. $\mathbb{E}(Y)=1/4$.
5. $\mathbb{E}(Y+U)=2U$.
