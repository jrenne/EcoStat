## Statement

Let $X$ have a continuous density $f$ and cumulative distribution function $F$. For $0<\alpha<1$, let $q_\alpha$ satisfy $F(q_\alpha)=\alpha$.

1. How can $f$ be obtained from $F$?
2. Derive the c.d.f. $F_\alpha$ of $X$ conditional on $X>q_\alpha$. State its value both below and above $q_\alpha$.
3. Derive the corresponding density $f_\alpha$.
