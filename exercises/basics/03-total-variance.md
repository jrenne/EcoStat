## Statement

Let $X$ be daily time spent on social media (in hours), and let $Y$ indicate age group. The population has the following characteristics:

| Age group | Population share | Mean (hours) | Variance (hours squared) |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| Under 30 | 0.6 | 4 | 1.5 |
| 30 or above | 0.4 | 2 | 0.5 |

(a) Calculate $\mathbb{E}[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y)]$.
(b) Calculate $\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}(X\mid Y)]$.
(c) Calculate $\operatorname{Var}[\mathbb{E}(X\mid Y)]$.
(d) Use the law of total variance to obtain $\operatorname{Var}(X)$.
